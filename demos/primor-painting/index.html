Whole-home interior

Moving in, selling, or just done with the old color everywhere. A full interior repaint, room by room, start to finish.

Not sure which sheen? A quick guide.

  • Flat / matteCeilings and low-traffic bedrooms. Hides roller marks and small wall flaws.
  • EggshellLiving rooms, dining rooms and hallways. Soft glow, easy to touch up.
  • SatinKitchens, baths and kids' rooms. Wipes clean without shining.
  • Semi-glossTrim, doors and cabinets. Tough, scrubbable and catches the light.
