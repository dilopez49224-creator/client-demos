Services

Everything on one sign.

The standard repair and maintenance a car needs, all under one roof so you don't have to chase it across town.

Oil & fluids

Oil and filter to the manufacturer's spec, plus coolant, brake and transmission fluid checks.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers and fluid. Squeal, grind or a soft pedal gets checked the same day where possible.

Tires

Mounting, balancing, rotation and plug repairs on a slow leak.

Check-engine diagnostics

Codes pulled, then the real cause tracked down before anything is replaced.

Battery & electrical

Battery, starter and alternator tested and replaced. No-start and dimming-light problems.

A/C & heat

Pressure check, leak test and recharge when the cabin stops getting cold. Heater and blower problems too.

Steering & suspension

Shocks, struts, tie rods, ball joints and wheel bearings. Clunks, wander and uneven tire wear.

Scheduled maintenance

Belts, hoses, filters, spark plugs and the mileage-interval services that keep a car out of the shop later.

Something not listed? Call 864-200-8969 and ask. Jobs that need a specialist get pointed to the right place.
