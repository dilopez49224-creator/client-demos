Everything the dining room does, plus the ways to bring it home or bring it to your event.

Why Towne

New in town, built for regulars.

  • A table that's actually held

    Call ahead and your table is waiting. No hovering by the door on a Friday night.

  • One kitchen, every occasion

    The same food at the table, in the takeout bag and on the catering tray.

  • A room for the whole party

    Private events seated together, with a set menu agreed in advance so the host can relax.

  • Pendleton's own

    A short walk or drive for the town, and an easy trip in from Clemson and Anderson.

"Towne" is the whole idea.

A neighborhood restaurant that knows your name by the second visit. New doors, old-fashioned hospitality.

Never miss a call — we text you back in seconds

In the middle of a dinner rush nobody can get to the phone, and the party of six calls the next restaurant. Missed-call text-back sends an instant text from Towne so the reservation lands with you.

  • Reply goes out seconds after a missed call
  • Sent in the Towne name, from the restaurant's number
  • Asks for party size and time so the host can confirm by text between seatings
