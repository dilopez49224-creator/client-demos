Popcorn ceiling, start to finish
  1. MaskFloors, walls and fixtures covered in plastic and drop cloths before anything comes down.
  2. Wet-scrapeThe texture is misted so it softens, then scraped off down to the drywall.
  3. Skim coatJoints, gouges and nail pops floated with compound and sanded flat.
  4. Prime & paintSealed with primer, then finished in flat ceiling paint so no roller marks show.
