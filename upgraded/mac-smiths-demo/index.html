On this corner since 1947

Mac's grandfather opened the store in 1947, selling to the farmers around Pendleton. His father ran a meat market here. Mac took it over in the early 1980s and started the grill.

  • Flaming BurgerTopped with jalapeños.
  • Wild West BurgerOnion rings, BBQ sauce, bacon and cheddar.
